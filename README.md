## 🌟 Live Features & Architecture

### 1. 📢 Campaign Landing Page (`/landing`)
- **Featured Theme**: *Natural Biohacking vs. Technology Biohacking: Ancient Tools & Modern Science* (Sep 19, 2026 @ Casa Mannabliss)
- **Container Split**: Highlights both the Free Outdoor Conscious Market and the Exclusive Indoor Workshop Activations
- **Direct SweatPals Integration**: 1-click checkout conversion links & WhatsApp community portals
- **Brand Asset Suite**: Embeds the Grok Imagine 2.0 dual-synthesis campaign poster and frame assets

### 2. 🏛️ Overview & Continuum (`/home`)
- **Living Foundation**: Interactive cards for the 4 Core Pillars (Ancient Wisdom, Frontier Science, Equitable Economics, Conscious Community)
- **Series Schedule**: Full timeline of Event 01 (Breathwork), Event 02 (Sound & Frequency), and Event 03 (Biohacking)
- **Radical Transparency Split**: Transparent breakdown showing 48% practitioner revenue distribution, 12% non-profit funds, and 12% Pay-It-Forward initiative

### 3. 🌿 Unified Partner Portal (`/portal`)
- **Role-based Application Flow**: Dual-mode interactive forms for **Conscious Vendors** (elixirs, artisans, tech) and **Practitioners** (sound healers, breathwork facilitators, panelists)
- **Direct Lead Routing**: Form submissions bypass third-party paywalls and store directly into the native CRM database

### 4. 🛡️ Sanctuary Operations Admin CRM (`/admin`)
- **Live Database Table**: Track, filter, review, approve, or decline partner applications
- **Subscriber Lead Capture**: Centralized list of RSVPs and newsletter signups
- **1-Click CSV Export**: Download structured lead and application data for external campaigns (Mailchimp, Klaviyo, WhatsApp broadcasts)
- **Real-Time Telemetry**: Tracks visitor interactions and AI chat queries

### 5. 🤖 Autonomous Sensorium AI Concierge
- **Native AI Bot**: Floating interactive assistant embedded on all pages
- **Instant Answers**: Resolves FAQs regarding event dates, venue parking, ticket pricing tiers, vendor requirements, and workshop schedules
